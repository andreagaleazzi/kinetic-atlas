## 26 Nov 2026

- [x] **Nicotine tab: optional blood-level curve (ng/mL), dotted, with its own axis.** Today the chart shows only mg in the body. Blood level is what the studies report and what drives felt effects, so a toggle adds it as a second curve.
  - **Evidence (Nov 2026, scripts not kept).** For pouches, body mg ÷ (2.6 L/kg × weight) already lands within ~10–15% of measured venous peaks (Azzopardi 2026 at 10/20/30 min, McEwan 2022 at 60 min): slow release lets tissue keep pace. Fast intake (cigarette, vape) spikes blood to about twice that, so a blood + tissue (two-compartment) model is needed. A central volume of 1.3 L/kg fits cigarettes (12–14 ng/mL vs 12–16 in the literature) and puts pouch peaks 18–37% high; smaller volumes overshoot pouches 1.4–5×. The larger uncertainty is upstream: blood exposure per extracted mg differs ~3× between Azzopardi and Mallock-Ohnesorg 2024, and clearance varies ±68% between people.
  - **Model.** Four inputs: total volume 2.6 L/kg (Benowitz/Hukkanen reviews), central volume 1.3 L/kg (our compromise fit, not a published value), initial blood half-life 7 min (Clin Pharmacokinet 2020 population model, 930 people), terminal half-life = profile `nicHalf`. Derive the transfer rates exactly from these four, so the tail always matches the body-mg curve and the existing ×0.7 / ×1.5 band works unchanged. Sanity check: at 70 kg, clearance should come out near the published 67 L/h. Weight comes from `state.profile.weight`.
  - **Code.** One closed-form function beside `pouchAmount`, same approach (sum of exponentials, convolved with the input): pouches use the two-phase release and `POUCH_KA`, other nicotine entries use today's even spread over `durationMin` and their own `ka`, short entries are a bolus. Guard against `ka` equal to either disposition rate, as `instantAmount` does. `buildSeries` adds a `nicBlood` series {v, low, high} beside `nicotine`; cost is about one more evaluation per point.
  - **UI.**
    - Toggle: one `aria-pressed` button, "Blood level", on the Nicotine tab only, in the toolbar slot the Combined tab uses for Relative / mg and Alcohol uses for `#lawRef`. Off by default; remembered in `localStorage` (try/catch), not in `state`, so exports and undo don't change.
    - Curve: dotted `[2,4]` 2px line in the `nic` hue, no area glow. Band drawn as for other compounds but at half alpha (`12`) so the two bands stay readable. Drawn after the mg series.
    - Axis: right-hand ng/mL ticks in a dimmed `nic` hue; `pad.r` grows from 12 to ~44 only while the toggle is on; three ticks below 500px. Fix the repeated-tick bug above for both axes in the same change.

- [x] **Pouch duration should change how much nicotine is absorbed.** Today it doesn't: `getFormDose` computes `dose = qty × mg × fraction`, and the pouch fraction is a fixed 30%. `durationMin` only spreads that same dose over time, so a 5-minute pouch and a 60-minute pouch absorb the same total.
  - Simple fix: when the user changes **Duration** on a pouch preset (group `'Oral nicotine'` only), update the **Estimated absorbed (%)** field with a saturating curve: `fraction = Fmax × (1 − e^(−dur/τ))`. With `Fmax ≈ 50%` and `τ ≈ 30 min` this gives ~14% at 10 min, ~32% at 30 min (≈ today's 30% default) and ~43% at 60 min. That's a few lines in the existing `entryDuration` input listener. The field stays editable as a manual override, and `getFormDose`, the stored entry and `validateEntry` don't change.
  - Don't apply it to patches (duration is the delivery window, already in delivered mg), cigarettes or vape (values are already absorbed estimates), or gum/lozenges (chewing pattern dominates; leave them for later).
  - Before shipping: find a cited source for `Fmax` and `τ` (pouch extraction vs. time-in-mouth studies), and add the formula and source to `#scienceDialog`, as the CLAUDE.md rule requires. Editing an old entry must keep its saved fraction: setting the field from code doesn't fire `input`, so it already does.
  - **Done:** On a pouch, changing Duration sets absorbed % to `60 × (1 − e^(−min/25))`: 20% at 10 min, 33% at 20, 42% at 30, 55% at 60. The values differ from the 50% / 30 min guess above because the extraction studies (Azzopardi 2026, Lunell 2020, Knopp 2022, Mallock-Ohnesorg 2024) put 30-minute extraction near 37–44%. Consequence: a new pouch entry defaults to 42% absorbed, not 30%. Saved entries keep their own %, and the field stays editable. Formula, parameters and citations are in `#sciPouch`. Three of the four studies are industry-funded and there is no data past 60 minutes.
